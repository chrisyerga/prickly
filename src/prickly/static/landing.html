The house

Hold to talk and say something like “dim the kitchen to 30% and lock the front door.” Whistle transcribes it, Needle picks from twelve typed tools, and the floor plan updates.

  • Compare pipeline (two model calls) with fused (one call, audio in and tool calls out)

Just the ears

Speech-to-text on its own, so you can see what transcription costs without Needle in the way. On slow CPUs, Needle accounts for most of the house demo's latency.

  • Seven languages with auto-detect, plus switchable word timestamps and keyword biasing
